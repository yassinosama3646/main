# Rebuild 100

A self-contained gym app for a 100-day recomposition challenge. No build step, no account, no server: open `index.html` on your phone, add it to the home screen, and it works offline after the first load. All data stays in the browser (use Settings → Backup weekly).

**Start:** Tuesday 13 Oct 2026 (Day 1) · **End:** Wednesday 20 Jan 2027 (Day 100)

## Running it

- **Phone / laptop, offline:** download the repo, open `index.html`. Chrome and Safari both keep the data between visits.
- **Hosted:** enable GitHub Pages on this repo (Settings → Pages → branch `main`, folder `/`) and open the URL on your phone → Share → Add to Home Screen.

## What the app does

| Tab | What you do there |
|---|---|
| **Today** | Morning weigh-in and waist, four daily habits, the planned workout with sets × reps, rest timer, last session beside every exercise, and an automatic "add 2.5 kg / hold / drop" suggestion. Log past days you forgot via Prev/Next. |
| **Plan** | 100-day calendar (done / missed / planned / deload / rest), the four phases with their rules, and all five splits with a "use this split" override. |
| **Progress** | Body-weight chart with 7-day average, weekly averages against the phase's target rate, estimated 1RM personal bests. |
| **Food** | Calories and macros for the current phase, recalculated from your latest weight, plus a calorie adjustment suggestion driven by your weekly trend. |
| **Guide** | Warm-up, progression rules, RPE, the chest and biceps strategy, fat-loss playbook, exercise library with cues and substitutions. |
| **Settings** | Start date, profile, split override, backup / restore, reset. |

## The plan

### Why Upper / Lower 4-day first

Studies that equate weekly sets find little difference between splits; what shows up repeatedly is that training each muscle twice a week beats once a week, and that four-day schedules get kept for months where six-day schedules get dropped. For a returning lifter, three recovery days a week also protect tendons that have not been loaded in a while. So: Upper/Lower until Day 56, then a five-day Upper/Lower/Push/Pull/Legs block for the final six weeks, with the option to stay on four days.

### Phases

| Days | Phase | Split | Effort | Calories | Weekly weight target |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1–21 | Rebuild | U/L 4-day, one set fewer per exercise | RPE 6–7 (3–4 reps left) | maintenance − 200 | −0.2 to −0.5 kg |
| 22–49 | Build | U/L 4-day, full sets | RPE 7–8 | maintenance | −0.2 to +0.2 kg |
| 50–56 | Deload | U/L, 2 sets, −20 % load | RPE 5–6 | maintenance − 100 | flat |
| 57–100 | Push | U/L + P/P/L 5-day, extra set on chest & biceps priority lifts | RPE 8–9 | maintenance + 200 | +0.2 to +0.4 kg |

Weekly pattern for the U/L block (Day 1 = Tuesday): **Tue** Upper A · **Wed** Lower A · **Thu** rest · **Fri** Upper B · **Sat** Lower B · **Sun / Mon** rest. Rest days: 8–10k steps, optional 20–30 min incline walk.

### Day 1 — Upper A (Tuesday 13 Oct 2026)

Warm-up: 5 min incline walk, band pull-aparts ×15, arm circles, then on the incline press: light ×12, 50 % ×8, 75 % ×4.

Phase 1 means **2 working sets** per exercise and stopping with 3–4 reps in reserve. The job today is to find your working weights and write them in the app, not to set records.

| # | Exercise | Sets × reps | Rest | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Incline dumbbell press (30°) | 2 × 8–10 | 2½ min | Main lift. Upper chest first while fresh. |
| 2 | Chest-supported row | 2 × 8–10 | 2 min | Pause 1 s with shoulder blades squeezed. |
| 3 | Machine chest press | 2 × 10–12 | 1½ min | |
| 4 | Lat pulldown | 2 × 10–12 | 1½ min | Elbows to the hips, control the way up. |
| 5 | Seated dumbbell shoulder press | 2 × 10–12 | 1½ min | |

About 50 minutes. Afterwards: 25–30 g protein within two hours, 8–10k steps for the day, bed by a time that gives you 7–9 hours.

### Progression

Double progression. Keep the weight until every set hits the top of the rep range with the phase's reps in reserve, then add 2.5 kg (upper body) or 5 kg (leg press / hack / hip thrust / RDL). The app compares each session with the last one and tells you when you have earned the jump.

### Nutrition (recalculated in the app from your latest weight)

At 76.7 kg: BMR ≈ 1,800 kcal, maintenance ≈ 2,700 kcal with 4–5 gym days and 8–10k steps.

| Phase | kcal | Protein | Fat | Carbs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Rebuild | ~2,500 | 160 g | 70 g | ~310 g |
| Build | ~2,700 | 160 g | 70 g | ~360 g |
| Deload | ~2,600 | 160 g | 70 g | ~335 g |
| Push | ~2,900 | 160 g | 70 g | ~410 g |

Adjust by 150 kcal at a time, only after two weeks of 7-day averages fall outside the phase target. Creatine 5 g daily. Alcohol zero in Phase 1.

### Chest and biceps, honestly

- A firm, rubbery disc directly under the nipple is glandular tissue from puberty. Diet and training do not shrink it; only an exam can confirm it and only surgery removes it. Soft, diffuse fullness is fat and responds to the deficit. Loose skin tightens slowly over years.
- Training lifts the chest visually by building the **upper** chest (incline press, low-to-high fly first on every upper day) and by lowering body fat. Flat bench and dips, which grow the lower chest, are deliberately not the focus.
- Biceps: three exposures a week across stretched (incline / Bayesian), mid-range (EZ-bar) and shortened (preacher) positions, plus hammer curls for the brachialis. 10–12 direct sets a week in Phases 1–2, 14–16 in Phase 3.

### What 100 days can deliver

With muscle memory, roughly +3–4 kg lean mass and −2–3 kg fat is a strong result: expect to finish around 78–80 kg, noticeably leaner, waist down 3–5 cm, incline press up 30–40 %, pull-ups unassisted. 82–85 kg lean is a 6–9 month target. Anything promising +7 kg of muscle in 14 weeks is promising fat.
